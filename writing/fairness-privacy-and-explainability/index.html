Privacy

February 2, 2026

I was interested by the different ways that users approach their privacy, as described in Kwesi et al. They said that some take it into their own hands, using VPNs and secure browsers to protect their personal data and prevent tracking. Others are resigned to the worst-case scenario that their data is already being harvested, can be used for violating purposes, and there is nothing they can do about it. Others trust that even if their data is in the possession of a company, that company won’t use it maliciously or in a way that violates the user’s trust. These are all reactions to a lack of transparency in privacy policies and big data practices. I am curious how people would react to a hypothetical complete transparency in big data practices. If we knew our trust was being violated, would we go out of our way to take appropriate personal measures? Would we make sure that the government was protecting us? Or would it just continue to happen?
