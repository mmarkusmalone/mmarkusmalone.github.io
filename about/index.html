About me

I am a growth engineer at Secureframe, where I work across all teams to build AI agents and the internal tools that help our company scale. I graduated from Duke University with a B.S. in Computer Science and a minor in Creative Writing. My work lives at the intersection of technical depth and creative problem solving, and I am drawn to systems that take real work off people's plates while keeping their trust.

I began coding as a freshman in high school and fell in love with it quickly, taking every computer science elective available and spending my free time building playful projects for friends and myself: an ASMR meditation web app, a cat-themed version of brick breaker, a geometric drawing app. That early infatuation grew into a lasting passion. At Duke it matured through coursework in algorithms, systems, and machine learning, and outside the classroom through personal projects and professional work.

At Secureframe, I built an internal sales room and agents behind it: one that triages the sales inbox and routes leads to reps, and a set of finance-operations agents that reconcile invoices and chase late payments. Before that, as an AI Intern at Splash Sports, I built production RAG applications and a modular framework for customizable agents. One of those projects, a Slack-integrated bug-fixing agent, was adopted across several teams and taught me that trustworthy AI is as much about people as it is about code. Earning users' trust took constant iteration, thoughtful design, and humility.

My personal projects reflect my love for blending engineering with creativity. I have built VADMap, an emotional mapping platform powered by a custom RoBERTa model; Dreamscape, a graph-based dream association tool; and Dance Partner, an application that tracks movement in real time and renders it as a skeleton and aura. Across all of them, I enjoy translating abstract ideas into intuitive, user-friendly experiences.

Human connection is the throughline in everything I do. At Duke, I was an Undergraduate Teaching Assistant for Design and Analysis of Algorithms, where I co-taught recitations and worked to build a classroom that encouraged curiosity, persistence, and confidence. My time as a coding tutor, FEMMES+ mentor, DTech member, and Duke Applied Machine Learning engineer strengthened my ability to collaborate across teams while supporting inclusive growth in STEM. At Secureframe, my work begins and ends with the internal stakeholders. I try my best to stay close to their actual needs and build products that truly make their work easier.

Beyond computer science, I read and think about philosophy, literature, and sociology, and I balanced my computer science semesters with humanities courses. Those explorations ground my technical work in broader questions about values, culture, and meaning.

In my free time, I was a DJ at WXDU and I am the founder of Allies With Advice, a mentorship platform for pediatric arthritis patients with a focus on digital accessibility. I also enjoy meditation, reading, and writing.
